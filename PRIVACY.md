# GratisCode Privacy Notes

This document explains what data the GratisCode waitlist site stores, how long it is kept, and how to request deletion.

## What we collect

We only collect information you voluntarily provide through the waitlist or sponsor forms:

- Name
- Email address
- City (optional)
- Builder type (student, indie builder, etc.)
- Current AI coding tool (optional)
- Why you are interested (optional)
- Sponsor details: company, budget range, target audience, message (optional)
- A hashed IP address for abuse prevention and rate limiting
- Consent timestamp

## What we do not collect

- Private code
- Prompts
- Repos or files
- Generated outputs
- Model reasoning
- Raw IP addresses (permanently)
- Raw user agent strings
- Tracking pixels or third-party analytics data

## Cookies and tracking

We do not use tracking pixels, Google Analytics, or third-party analytics by default. We do not set cookies unless strictly necessary for security or functionality. If we add cookies, this document and the site will be updated.

## How we use data

- Manage the waitlist and sponsor leads
- Contact you about closed access or sponsorship
- Prevent abuse and spam
- Improve the service

## Data retention

Data is retained until you request deletion or until the project winds down.

## Request deletion

Email **usegratiscode@protonmail.com** with the subject:

```
Delete my GratisCode data
```

We will process your request as soon as possible.

## Contact

For privacy questions, email **usegratiscode@protonmail.com**.
